## Full text

### About AQR Capital Management

AQR is a global investment management firm built at the intersection of financial theory and practical application. We strive to deliver superior, long-term results for our clients by looking past market noise to identify and isolate what matters most, and by developing ideas that stand up to **rigorous testing**. Our focus on practical insights and analysis has made us leaders in alternative and traditional strategies since 1998.

At AQR, our employees share a common spirit of academic excellence, intellectual honesty and an unwavering commitment to seeking the truth. We're determined to know what makes financial markets tick – and we'll ask every question and challenge every assumption. We recognize and respect the power of collaboration, and we believe transparency and openness to new ideas leads to innovation.

### The Team

Technology is central to AQR's success. As a Senior Software Engineer in Portfolio Implementation, you will lead the design and delivery of complex, mission-critical systems that turn investment ideas into reality. Working closely with researchers, portfolio managers, and engineering peers, you will drive large-scale technical initiatives while mentoring engineers and raising the bar for engineering excellence across the team.

### Your Role

As a Senior Software Engineer in Portfolio Implementation, you will operate with broad ownership and technical authority across core investment systems, including:

- Leading the design and implementation of portfolio implementation platforms that **consume research outputs, perform constrained optimizations, and generate orders**
- Owning and evolving distributed, microservice-based systems that operate reliably at scale
- Lead architectural design for high-performance, high-volume, highly available systems, making informed trade-offs across performance, flexibility, and maintainability
- Delivering complex, cross-cutting initiatives that span multiple services, teams
- Providing technical leadership to small teams (typically 2–5 engineers), including task planning, design reviews, and execution oversight
- Mentoring and coaching engineers through **code reviews**, design discussions, and hands-on guidance
- Partnering with a global team of researchers, portfolio managers and engineers to deliver robust, production-quality solutions on time

### What You'll Bring

- Degree in Computer Science or a related technical field
- 7+ years of professional software engineering experience, with deep expertise in Java
- Strong experience using **Python in production or research-adjacent environments**
- Proven ability to design, build, and operate large-scale, distributed systems
- Experience operating microservice-based architectures, using technologies such as Java, Elasticsearch, Redis, RabbitMQ / AMQ, and **relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server)**
- Strong command of **engineering best practices, including code quality, design documentation, peer code reviews, automated testing, and test coverage**
- Experience operating, tuning, and profiling high-performance systems in production environments
- Experience with **observability and monitoring platforms** (e.g., New Relic) and using metrics to drive reliability and performance improvements
- Ability to lead technical change, manage risk, and drive initiatives to completion in complex environments
- Experience contributing to requirements capture, technical specification, and work breakdown
- Ability to estimate work, plan delivery, and manage technical trade-offs
- Interest in financial markets and the investment process

### Who You Are

- A strong technical leader who takes ownership of outcomes, not just implementations
- Comfortable driving large, ambiguous initiatives from concept through delivery
- Effective at mentoring and developing engineers, setting clear expectations and providing constructive feedback
- Able to balance hands-on contribution with leadership and oversight
- Collaborative, thoughtful, and effective working across disciplines and geographies
- Committed to intellectual integrity, transparency, and openness
