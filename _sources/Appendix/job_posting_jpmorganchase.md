## Full text

### Data & AI

JPMorganChase's data and AI ecosystem spans centralized platforms and federated teams that embed with businesses to deliver outcomes at scale. The Chief Data & Analytics Office (CDAO) sets firmwide strategy, governance, risk controls, and tooling for data and AI—covering **data management and quality**, model risk policies and review frameworks, and platform products like LLM Suite for secure AI assistance, analytics, and automation across lines of business.

Embedded Data & AI teams within each business (e.g., payments, consumer banking, markets) deliver end to end solutions using approved tools: they **engineer data, build and deploy models, and monitor them in production** to solve real problems such as fraud detection, credit risk, trading and liquidity optimization, customer personalization, and operational efficiency. Opportunities span data management, analytics and AI/ML—working with large datasets, **feature pipelines, and production systems** alongside product and tech partners.

### What you'll do

You'll collaborate with global experts to deliver real impact by building end-to-end solutions across data, analytics, governance, and AI/ML—translating business goals into measurable outcomes for clients and customers. In data-focused work, you'll **design and run scalable platforms and pipelines**, define knowledge architecture, build intuitive dashboards, and help ensure strong governance, privacy, and **data quality standards**. In AI-focused work, you'll integrate diverse datasets, develop and deploy machine learning solutions, and run experiments tied to key business outcomes using modern Data & AI tools such as **Python, SQL, AWS, Copilot, Snowflake, Databricks**, Fusion, and Large Language Models (LLMs). Across all roles, you'll work in agile, cross-functional teams, receive mentorship and training, and contribute ideas from day one in a culture that champions innovation, growth, and impact.

### Who we're looking for

**Valued qualities:**

- Candidates are pursuing a bachelor's or master's degree in quantitative or technical disciplines such as Data Science, Machine Learning, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, Economics, Applied Math, Operations Research, Business Analytics, or related fields.

**Key skills:**

- Knowledge of machine learning and data science concepts, techniques, and tools; experience working with large, diverse datasets is a plus.
- For AI-focused roles, proficiency in Python and familiarity with common analytical stacks and platforms (Jupyter, AWS, Spark, PyTorch, R). **SQL and visualization tools** like Tableau for data focused roles.
- Understanding of **data management and governance, including data platforms, pipelines, models, taxonomies, metadata, lineage, privacy, and regulatory compliance**.
- Strong quantitative and analytical problem solving skills, with the ability to design experiments and deliver measurable outcomes (e.g., KPIs, uplift, ROI).
- Clear written and verbal communication skills to translate complex technical work for business stakeholders and collaborate in agile, cross functional teams.

### What we offer

Your professional growth and development will be supported throughout the program via project work related to your academic and professional interests, mentorship, engaging speaker series with senior leaders, and more.

**Training:** Through research and hands-on work experience, you'll develop solutions and technology that help solve the world's most interesting financial problems, and improve and protect our customer and client experiences every day. You'll be supported by your teammates, tutors, and mentors throughout the experience.

**Career progression:** The specialized knowledge and skills gained through the program will prepare you for a successful career at the firm. Top-performing intern candidates may receive a full-time offer.

### Our opportunities

**Turn Data into Business Impact.** Join a team that sits at the intersection of data, technology, and business strategy. As a Data & AI Analyst, you'll work on high-impact challenges that influence decisions for millions of customers and operations across one of the world's largest financial institutions.

**Data Engineering & Data Products**

- **Build and optimize scalable data pipelines and products** that power critical business decisions.
- Design solutions that improve **data quality, accessibility, governance, and discoverability**.
- Help create the data foundations that enable advanced analytics and AI across the firm.

**Analytics & Insights**

- Transform complex data into actionable insights using modern analytics and visualization tools.
- Develop **dashboards and reporting solutions** that enable leaders to make better, faster decisions.
- Apply statistical analysis and experimentation to measure outcomes and drive continuous improvement.

**Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**

- Develop and deploy machine learning and generative AI solutions that address real business challenges.
- Integrate structured and unstructured data to create predictive and intelligent applications.
- Evaluate, test, and improve AI models while ensuring responsible and compliant use of emerging technologies.

**Strategy & Problem Solving**

- Partner with business, product, and technology teams to solve complex challenges.
- Translate business needs into data-driven solutions that deliver measurable impact.
- Contribute to initiatives shaping the future of Data & AI across the firm.

**Growth & Development**

- Receive structured training, mentorship, and hands-on learning from industry experts.
- Build technical, analytical, and leadership skills through real project experience from day one.
- Develop a broad understanding of financial services while building expertise in one of the fastest-growing fields in technology.
