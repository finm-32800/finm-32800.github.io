# FTSFR: The Slides

The deck Jeremy uses in week 6 to go through the paper behind the
[Financial Time Series Forecasting Repository](../Week3/ftsfr.md), one slide per
image. It is the September 2025 talk, and it predates the published
[OFR working paper](https://www.financialresearch.gov/working-papers/2026/08/25/time-series-forecasting-methods-financial-markets/):
where the two differ (the deck benchmarks 24 methods, the paper 12), the
paper is the reference. The deck as a PDF:
[slides_ftsfr.pdf](./assets/slides_ftsfr.pdf).
